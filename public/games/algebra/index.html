Algebra Lab

Solve equations, spot patterns and read straight lines, one small step at a time.

How to play
  • Pick a topic along the top, then a level. Each round has ten questions.
  • Every answer comes with a short explanation, right or wrong, so you can see how it works.
  • Get 5 right for one star, 7 for two and 9 or 10 for three. Stars are saved on this device.
  • Typed answers can be whole numbers, decimals or fractions like 1/2. Use a minus sign for negative numbers.
  • Explore opens a hands-on panel: solve an equation on a balance scale step by step, or build your own straight line with sliders.
  • Online race: you and a friend get the same ten questions. Most right wins; if it's a tie, the faster player wins.
