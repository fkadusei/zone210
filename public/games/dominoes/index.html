Dominoes

Match the ends of the chain and be first to play all your tiles.

Mode
Level
Match

How to play
  • Each player gets 7 tiles, and 14 stay in the boneyard. The player with the highest double starts.
  • On your turn, play a tile that has the same number of spots as one end of the chain.
  • No tile that fits? Draw from the boneyard until you find one. When it is empty you pass.
  • The first player to play all their tiles wins the round and scores the spots left in the other hand. If nobody can play, the lowest hand wins the difference.
  • In a match, the first to 100 points wins.
