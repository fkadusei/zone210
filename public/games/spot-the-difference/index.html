Spot the Difference

The two pictures look the same, but they aren't. Tap every difference.

Players

How to play
  • The two pictures are almost the same. Find the differences: something missing or extra, a different colour, a swapped object, something flipped, bigger or smaller, or (on Hard) moved.
  • Tap the difference in either picture. Wrong taps are counted, so look carefully!
  • Stuck? Tap 💡 Hint for a glowing clue. Finish with no hints and few wrong taps for three stars.
  • Online race: you and a friend get the same puzzle. Whoever spots each difference first gets the point.
  • Keyboard: Tab to a picture, move the crosshair with the arrow keys (Shift for small steps) and press Enter.
