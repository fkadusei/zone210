Yote

A traditional capture game: jump to capture, and every capture takes a bonus piece.

How to play
  • The board has 5 rows and 6 columns. Each player starts with 12 pieces in hand and the board empty.
  • On your turn do ONE of these: place a piece from your hand on any empty square, slide one of your pieces one square up, down, left or right to an empty square, or capture.
  • To capture, jump one of your pieces over an enemy piece next to it (up, down, left or right) onto the empty square straight beyond. The jumped piece is removed.
  • Every capture earns a bonus: also remove any other enemy piece of your choice from the board. If they have none on the board, take one from their hand instead.
  • You win when your opponent has no pieces left (on the board or in hand), or can't move. Fifty turns without a capture, or the same position three times, is a draw.
