How to play
  • Drag each piece to where it belongs. When it is close, it snaps into place.
  • 👻 Ghost picture shows a faint copy of the picture on the board. 🧱 Edges first hides the middle pieces until the border is done.
  • Keyboard: Tab to a piece and press Enter to put it in its place.
  • Online race: you and a friend get the same picture, cut the same way. Whoever finishes first wins.
