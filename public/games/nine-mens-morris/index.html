Nine Men's Morris

An ancient game of mills: line up three to capture, and leave your opponent with no moves.

How to play
  • Each side has 9 pieces. Make a "mill": three of your pieces in a straight line along a line of the board. Each mill lets you remove one of your opponent's pieces.
  • You can't remove a piece that is in a mill unless every one of their pieces is in a mill.
  • Phase 1: take turns placing your pieces on empty points. Phase 2: once all are placed, slide a piece along a line to an empty neighbouring point.
  • When a player is down to 3 pieces, they can "fly": move any piece to any empty point.
  • You lose when you are reduced to 2 pieces, or when you can't move. The same position three times in a row is a draw.
