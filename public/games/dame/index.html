Dame

The Ghanaian draughts. Men capture backwards, kings fly, and a missed capture can be huffed.

How Dame is played here
  • Light moves first. Men step one square diagonally forward and capture in all four directions.
  • Kings fly: they slide any distance along a diagonal, and after jumping a piece they can land on any empty square beyond it.
  • You may pick any capture route, but once you start a capture you must finish the chain.
  • Huffing: if you skip a capture you could have made, your opponent may remove the piece that could have captured. Choose Forced if you'd rather the game makes you capture.
  • Reach the far side to crown a king. You win when your opponent has no pieces or no moves.
