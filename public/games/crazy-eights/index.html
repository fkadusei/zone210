Crazy Eights

Match the suit or the number, and be first to empty your hand. Eights are wild.

Mode
Level
Opponents
Match

How to play
  • Everyone gets 7 cards (5 with three or four players). One card is turned face up to start.
  • On your turn, play a card that matches the top card's suit or its number.
  • Eights are wild. Play one any time, and call the suit that comes next.
  • Nothing to play? Draw cards until you can. If the pile runs out, the discards are shuffled back in.
  • The first player to empty their hand wins the round and scores the cards left in the others' hands: an 8 is worth 50, a face card or 10 is worth 10, an ace is 1, and the rest are face value.
  • In a match, the first to 100 points wins.
