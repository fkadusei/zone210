How to play
  • Tap a tile in your rack, then tap an empty square to put it down. Tap a placed tile to take it back.
  • Words go left to right or top to bottom, and every new word must touch tiles already on the board. The first word covers the ★.
  • Coloured squares multiply a letter (blue) or a whole word (pink and red). They only count the turn a tile lands on them.
  • Use all 7 tiles in one turn for a 50 point bonus. The blank tile is a wild card worth 0 points.
  • Can't play? Swap tiles (while the bag has 7 or more) or pass. The game ends when someone uses all their tiles with the bag empty, or everyone passes twice in a row. Tiles left in your rack count against you.
  • Words are checked against the ENABLE word list, a free Scrabble-style dictionary.
