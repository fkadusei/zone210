How to play
  • Tap a number, then an operation, then a second number. The two numbers are replaced by the answer.
  • Keep going until one number is left. If it is 24, you win.
  • Every puzzle can be solved. Some need fractions on the way, for example 8 ÷ (3 − 8 ÷ 3).
  • Keyboard: press 1–4 to pick a card, then + − * /, then another card. Backspace undoes.
  • Sprint: solve as many as you can in 90 seconds. Skipping is free.
