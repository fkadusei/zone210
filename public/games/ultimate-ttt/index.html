How to play
  • The big board is made of nine small boards. Win a small board by getting three in a row inside it, and it becomes yours.
  • The cell you pick inside a small board decides which small board your opponent must play in next. Play in the top-right cell and they must play in the top-right board.
  • If you are sent to a board that is already won or full, you may play in any open board.
  • Win the game by claiming three small boards in a row on the big board. If every board is decided without a line, the player with more boards wins.
