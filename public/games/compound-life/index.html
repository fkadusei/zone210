Compound Life

Create a family, give them a home, and help them live their best lives: work, school, meals, fun and friends.

What happened

    How to play
    • Tap a family member to pick them, then tap furniture to see what they can do (cook, sleep, watch TV, take a bath…). Tap another person to chat.
    • Keep their needs green: hunger, energy, fun, hygiene, social life and bladder. Happy people do better at work and school.
    • Grown-ups go to work on weekdays and earn money; children go to school. A good mood leads to promotions and good grades.
    • With Free will on, people look after themselves when you aren't giving orders.
    • Use Buy to add furniture (tap an item, then tap the floor), or to move and sell what you have. Rent is paid every morning.
    • Your family is saved on this device, one for each home.
