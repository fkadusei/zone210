Ribbon Sort

Move the ribbon rolls until every pole holds just one color.

Puzzle
Players

How to play
  • Tap a pole to lift its top rolls, then tap the pole to put them on. You can also drag from one pole to another.
  • Rolls can only go onto an empty pole, or onto a roll of the same color, and a pole holds only so many.
  • All the rolls of one color on top move together, as many as fit.
  • You win when every pole holds rolls of just one color (or nothing).
  • Stuck? Undo as many moves as you like, or tap Hint for a good next move. Every puzzle can be solved.
  • Each roll has a little shape as well as its color, so you can tell them apart by either.
  • Keyboard: number keys choose a pole (1 to 9), Esc lets go, Z undoes.
  • Online race: you and a friend get the same puzzle. Whoever sorts it first wins.
