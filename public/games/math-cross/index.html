How to play
  • Every row and column of tiles is an equation, like 8 + 4 = 12. The empty squares are yours to fill.
  • Tap a number in the bank, then tap an empty square. Or tap a square first, then a number. On a keyboard, click a square and type the number.
  • Tap a placed number to send it back. Arrow keys move between squares, Backspace clears one.
  • Equations turn green when they are correct. The puzzle is solved when they all are.
