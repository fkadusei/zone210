Chinese Checkers

Race all ten of your marbles across the star to the opposite corner, hopping over anything in your way.

How to play
  • Each player has 10 marbles in one corner of the star. The aim is to move all of them into the opposite corner. The first to do it wins.
  • On your turn move one marble: either one step to a neighbouring empty hole, or hop over an adjacent marble (yours or anyone's) into the empty hole right behind it.
  • You can keep hopping in the same turn. Look for chains of hops: a ladder of marbles lets one marble cross the board in a single move.
  • Play with 2, 3, 4 or 6 players. Online games are for 2 players.
  • If the only thing stopping you from filling your goal is an opposing marble that is trapped inside it, you win.
