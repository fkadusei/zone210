How to play
  • Drag back from the slingshot and let go. The dots show where your bird will start to fly.
  • Keyboard: click the game (or Tab to it), then ↑ ↓ aim higher or lower, ← → pull less or more (hold Shift for small steps), and Space or Enter fires. Press Space again while a bird is flying to use its power. Your aim is kept for the next shot.
  • Knock out every monkey to win the mangoes back. Birds you don't need earn bonus points and stars.
  • Kwaku is a steady shot. Tap the screen while Ama is flying to split into three, while Diego is flying to dash, and while Yuki is flying to dive straight down. Yuki is heavy enough to smash stone.
  • Wood breaks, clay pots shatter easily, and stone is tough.
  • Levels with a friend: play the adventure levels together online. You take turns firing the birds, win or lose together, and see who scored more. The player who makes the room picks the level.
  • Duel: each side has a fort with three monkeys. Take turns firing, and the first to knock out all of the other side's monkeys wins. Play the computer, a friend on this device, or a friend online.
