Sprouts

Draw a line between two spots, add a new spot in the middle, and be the last player who can move.

Spots

Spot colours show how many lines they can still take: 3 2 1 none.

How to play
  • Start with a few spots. On your turn draw a line from one spot to another spot, or from a spot back to itself (a loop). Then add a new spot anywhere on your new line (the game does this for you, in the middle).
  • Lines may never cross each other or themselves, and can't pass through a spot.
  • Every spot can have at most three lines. The new spot you add already has two (the line goes through it), so it has one left.
  • The player who can't make a move loses, so make the last move to win. A game of 3 spots lasts at most 8 moves.
  • How to move: press on a spot, drag to another spot and let go. You can steer around other lines (drag back along your line to undo). Or just tap one spot then another, and the game draws the shortest line for you. Tap the same spot twice to draw a loop.
