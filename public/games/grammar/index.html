Grammar & Words

Build better sentences: grammar, punctuation, word meanings and reading.

How to play
  • Pick a topic along the top, then a level. Each round has ten questions.
  • Grammar: tap the word that is the noun, verb, adjective and so on, or choose the form that fits.
  • Punctuation: choose the sentence that is written correctly.
  • Vocabulary: words that mean the same, opposites, and words that are easy to mix up.
  • Sentences: tap the jumbled words in the right order. Tap a word in your sentence to take it back out.
  • Reading: read a short passage, then answer a question about it.
  • Every answer comes with a short explanation. Get 5 right for one star, 7 for two and 9 or 10 for three.
  • Online race: you and a friend get the same ten questions. Most right wins; if it's a tie, the faster player wins.
