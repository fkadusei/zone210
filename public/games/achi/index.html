Achi

A classic game of skill: place four pieces, then slide them to make three in a row.

How to play
  • Each player has 4 pieces. Take turns placing them on any empty point of the board.
  • Once all 8 pieces are down, take turns sliding one of your pieces along a line to an empty neighbouring point.
  • The first player to get three of their pieces in a straight line wins, even during the placing phase. The lines run along the rows, the columns and both diagonals.
  • If you can't move, you lose. If the same position comes up three times, the game is a draw.
