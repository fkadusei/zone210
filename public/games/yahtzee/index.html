Yahtzee

Roll five dice up to three times, keep the ones you like, and fill the scorecard.

Mode
Level

How to play
  • On your turn, roll the five dice. You may roll up to three times: after each roll, tap the dice you want to keep and the rest are rolled again.
  • Then you must write your dice into one category on the scorecard (tap a row to score it). Each category is used once, so after 13 turns the card is full. A category that doesn't fit can be taken as a 0.
  • Upper section: add up the dice showing that number. Score 63 or more in the upper section for a 35-point bonus (three of each number does it).
  • Lower section: three or four of a kind (add all the dice), full house (3 + 2) 25, small straight (4 in a row) 30, large straight (5 in a row) 40, Yahtzee (five alike) 50, and chance (any dice, added up).
  • Roll a second Yahtzee after scoring 50 in the Yahtzee box and you get a 100-point bonus, and the roll can be used in any open category (a full house or straight scores its full value).
  • The highest total wins.
