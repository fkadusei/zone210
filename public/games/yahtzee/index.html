Yahtzee

Roll five dice up to three times a turn and fill your scorecard.

Mode
Level

How to play
  • On your turn, roll the five dice. Tap the dice you want to keep, then roll the rest again. You get up to three rolls.
  • Then pick a box on the scorecard to score your dice in. Every box can be used once, and a box you can't fill scores 0.
  • Top section: add up only the dice that show that number. Score 63 or more there and you get a 35 point bonus.
  • Bottom section: three or four of a kind score all the dice; a full house (3 + 2) scores 25; a small straight (four in a row) scores 30; a large straight (five in a row) scores 40; five of a kind is a YAHTZEE worth 50; chance scores all the dice.
  • Roll another Yahtzee after scoring 50 in the Yahtzee box and you earn a 100 point bonus, and the dice can fill any open box (a full house or straight scores in full).
  • After 13 turns the highest total wins. Playing solo? Try to beat your best score.
