Race your pawn to the far side, and slow your opponent down with walls.

How to play
  • Blue starts at the bottom and wants to reach the top row; Red starts at the top and wants the bottom row. Whoever gets there first wins.
  • Each turn you do one of two things: move your pawn one square up, down, left or right, or place a wall (each player has ten).
  • A wall is two squares long and blocks movement between squares. Walls can't overlap or cross, and you may never wall a pawn in completely: there must always be a way through for both players.
  • If the other pawn is next to you, you can jump over it. If a wall or the edge is right behind it, you can step diagonally past it instead.
  • To place a wall, press Place wall, point at the groove between squares, use Rotate (or the R key) to turn it, and click to place it. On a phone, tap once to preview and tap again (or press Place it) to confirm.
  • Tip: walls cost you a turn, so use them where they make the other player's route much longer.
