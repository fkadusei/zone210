How to play
  • Bring it to life: tap Draw a new thing, draw one thing (a fish, a ball, a cloud) and tap Done. Then choose how it moves and what else it does.
  • Follow my path: trace a line with your finger and your drawing will travel along it. If you end where you started, it goes round and round.
  • Tools: the pen draws freely. Shapes draws a line, rectangle, oval, triangle, diamond, star or heart: press and drag. Fill colours the inside of something you have drawn: pick a colour and tap inside it. Stickers stamps a ready-made person, pet, animal or thing: tap where it should go. Drawn people and Drawn characters (Santa, a wizard, a pirate, a mermaid and more) come in six skin tones and six hair colours, and you can choose the colour of their clothes: tap a person, then pick its colours. Each one keeps its own colours, so changing one does not change the others. Drawn animals (a dog, cat, lion, elephant, penguin, fish and more) can be any two colours you like. So can the Drawn things that go (a car, train, rocket, boat and more: their wheels turn when they travel), Drawn nature (trees, flowers, the sun, clouds, a volcano) and Drawn things (a house, a castle, toys, food, a clock and more). There are emoji characters and animals too. To take a sticker or a person away, choose the Eraser and tap it. The size buttons change how thick the line is, or how big the sticker.
  • Text puts words on the page. Type them in the box, pick one of eight fonts, a colour and a size, then tap where they should go. You can add as many pieces of text as you like, each in its own font. Press Enter in the box for a second line. Tap words that are already there to change them; use Move to slide, resize or turn them, and the Eraser to take them away.
  • Move lets you pick up anything in a drawing: tap a line, shape, sticker or person, then drag it. Drag the round handle at its corner to make it bigger or smaller, and the round handle above it to turn it. There are buttons for both as well. Tick Everything on the page to move or resize the whole page at once.
  • Tap a drawing to choose it. Drag it to move it, drag the round handle at its corner to resize it, and drag the handle above it to turn it. Each drawing can move in its own way.
  • Joints make your own drawings bend. Draw the body, and each arm, leg, tail or wing as its own line. Then choose Joints and tap an arm or a leg: a pin appears where it joins the body, and the part swings from the pin. Drag the pin if it is in the wrong place. Pin a second leg and it swings the opposite way, so the two take turns. Add elbow or knee makes the part bend a second time: tap the arm or leg part-way along and a blue pin appears there. It folds to one side only, as a real elbow or knee does; tick Bends the other way if it folds the wrong way. You can add more than one: a tail, a trunk or a tentacle with several curls like a whip. Drew it all in one line? Draw a ring round the part that should move. The line is cut where it crosses your ring, and the part inside gets a pin. A ring also gathers several lines into one part, such as an arm with its hand. Add lines to it lets a hand or a foot swing with its arm or leg. Tap a pin or a pinned part to choose it, then change how far it swings or remove it.
  • Stickers you stamp apart from each other become separate drawings when you tap Done, so each can be moved by itself. If one drawing holds several things standing apart, choose it and tap Split apart. To keep drawings together, choose one, tap Join, then tap each drawing that should join it. Joined drawings are moved, resized, turned, copied and deleted together, but each keeps its own way of moving when you press Play: tap one of them to choose how that one moves. To make a joined group travel as one, tap the member the others should follow and tick The group travels together: they all go where it goes, each still doing its own bounce, spin or wiggle. Split apart sets them free again.
  • Press Play to watch everything move together.
  • Moving by themselves: when you press Play, the pictures from the sticker sets come alive. Hand-drawn people step and swing their arms, the Walking animals stride, tails wag, wings flap, the robot waves, the ghost floats and the dragon beats its wings. Emoji stickers move in their own way too: animals waddle and hop, birds flap, fish wriggle, cars rumble, boats rock and trees sway. Give something Side to side, Round in a circle or Follow my path to see it walk. Things that stay still in real life, like a house, stay still. Your own pen drawings bend where you put joints.
  • Flipbook: draw a page, tap New page, and draw the next one a tiny bit different. The faint picture shows the page before. Press Play to flip through them.
  • Figures take a step on each page: in the flipbook, the people, animals and stickers take their next pose every time the page turns, about eight pages to a full stride. Copy a page, slide the runner along a little with Move, and do it again: the runner walks across your book with moving legs. A figure that stays in the same place just waves or wags. Parts you have pinned with Joints swing one step per page too. Untick the box for plain still pictures.
  • Fill in to the next page: the game can draw the pages in between for you. Make a page, copy it, and on the copy slide, resize or turn things with Move. Go back to the first of the two, choose 1, 2 or 3, and tap the button: new pages appear between them with everything part of the way along, so the movement is smoother. It works for things that were copied from one page to the next, not for things drawn again by hand.
  • Copy page is the quick way: copy, then change just one small thing. Delete page removes the page you are on; you can put it straight back if you change your mind.
  • Each page can have its own background. Pick one for the page you are on, or tap Use on every page to give them all the same one. A new page starts with the background of the page before it.
  • Music: choose one of 24 tunes, such as Adventure, Spooky, Pirate or Rock. It plays while your animation plays. Tap Listen to try one first. Your scene and your flipbook each keep their own tune.
  • Press Play and your pages become a little book that flips by itself. To turn a page yourself, drag it across: left to go on, right to go back. Let go and it lands. Or slide the thumb slider to go through the pages one by one.
  • Your work is saved on this device. Save video records a few seconds of your animation, with its music and sounds, to keep or share.
