    How to play
    • Pass & play: one person draws on this device while everyone else guesses out loud. The drawer taps who got it right. Pass the device on for the next turn.
    • Online: you and a friend take turns drawing. Your friend sees your drawing live and types guesses.
    • No letters, numbers or talking while you draw! Pointing is fine.
    • A correct guess scores for both the drawer and the guesser, and faster guesses score more.
