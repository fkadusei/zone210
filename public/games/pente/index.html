How to play
  • Players take turns placing a stone on any intersection. Black goes first.
  • Win by five in a row (horizontal, vertical or diagonal), or by capturing five pairs of the opponent's stones.
  • Capturing: if you flank exactly two of your opponent's stones in a line with your own stones on both ends (you, them, them, you), you remove those two stones. Placing your own pair between two enemy stones is safe.
  • Tournament rule (on by default, to balance the first move): Black's first stone goes in the centre, and Black's second stone must be at least three points away from the centre.
  • Tip: an open row of three is a threat, because it can become an open four that can't be stopped. Watch your pairs too: a pair with an enemy stone at one end can be captured.
