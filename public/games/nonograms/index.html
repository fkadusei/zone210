How to play
  • The numbers beside each row and above each column tell you the runs of filled squares in that line, in order. “3 1” means three filled squares together, then at least one gap, then one more.
  • Start with the big numbers: a 5 in a 5-square line fills the whole line! Mark squares you know are empty with ✗.
  • Drag to fill or mark several squares at once. A clue turns green when its line matches it.
  • Every puzzle can be solved by logic, with no guessing.
  • Keyboard: arrows move, Space fills, X marks. On a computer, right-click marks.
  • Online race: you and a friend get the same puzzle. Whoever finishes first wins.
